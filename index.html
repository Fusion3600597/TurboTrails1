How to race

One player

  • W↑ Accelerate
  • S↓ Brake / reverse
  • AD←→ Steer
  • Shift Hop & drift
  • Space Use item
  • R Recover to track
  • Esc Pause · M Mute

Two players · split

  • P1 WASD · LShift drift · Space item · R reset
  • P2 ↑←↓→ · RShift drift · / item · . reset
  • Both players race the same 8-kart field.
  • Pick P2's racer by tapping the P2 slot, then a portrait.
  • Split-screen races use Quick Race.

Race craft

  • Drift boost. Hold Shift and steer. Let go after the sparks turn orange or purple.
  • Coins. Each coin raises your top speed. Ten is the cap.
  • Items. Red shells chase the racer ahead. Bananas stay where you drop them.
  • Boost pads. Hit the glowing chevrons for a free burst of speed.
  • Off-road. Grass and snow kill your speed — stay on the tarmac.

Touch

  • Steer with the left pads.
  • Hold Drift through corners.
  • Tap Item to fire.
  • Acceleration is automatic.
